Rooh ਰੂਹ

Outlines root cross-sections automatically, lets you fix them by hand, and gives you the traits as Excel. Use a laptop or desktop in Chrome or Firefox (you need a keyboard and mouse).

jagdeep2021--rooh.modal.run the website; the desktop app is the same page

Steps

  1. Ask for access (once)

    Type your name and email, click Ask for access. Jagdeep approves it, then you reload the page. (Not needed in the desktop app.) Keep using the same browser: it remembers you. A private window or cleared cookies means asking again.

  2. Make a project

    Click + at the top, type a name (e.g. the experiment ID), Create.

  3. Add images

    Folder… uploads a whole folder; each sub-folder becomes a group (genotype, treatment) in the results. Files… adds single images. TIF, PNG or JPG. Don't close the tab until the upload finishes.

  4. Outline

    Uploading does not start this: when the upload finishes, click Outline. The server finds cells, stele, vessels and root edge, about 10 s per image. If you add more images later, click it again; it only does the new ones and leaves your edits alone.

  5. Set the scale

    Type the µm per pixel in the box, or click 📏 (or M), drag along the scale bar and type its length. Then All for every image, or Image for this one only. Without a scale, areas come out in pixels.

  6. Check every image

    Pick what to fix in the Edit panel (or press its number), then: drag draws, click adds, hold ⌘/Ctrl to remove.

    • 1 Cell: click inside to fill
    • 2 Vessel (metaxylem)
    • 3 Stele: draw the outline
    • 4 Sector: leave out damage
    • 5 Root: draw the outline
    • 6 Aerenchyma lacunae

    Get the stele right first: cell file numbers and positions are counted out from it. When the image looks right, Save + next (Shift+S). In the image list ● = checked, ○ = not yet.

  7. Make results, then Download

    Make results builds the Excel files and group graphs; Download gives you a zip. Re-run it after any more edits; never edit the Excel by hand.

Rules that matter for the numbers

  • Leave out technical damage only (torn, folded, oblique, out of focus, debris). Aerenchyma, collapsed or odd cells are biology: keep them, they get measured.
  • To leave out damage: Sector tool, drag around the stele from one side of the damage to the other, and pick the reason. ⌘/Ctrl-click a grey arc to bring it back.
  • Aerenchyma: orange = counted. If an orange patch is really cells the outlining missed, fix it with the Cell tool rather than un-counting it.
  • Don't delete a project (🗑) without asking Jagdeep.
